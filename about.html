More Than a Haircut.
A Cairo Institution.

For years, Hany & Belal Hairdresser has stood as Dokki's most trusted name in men's grooming. What began as a commitment to craft has grown into a community of loyal clients who return not just for the cut — but for the experience.

Every chair in our salon carries the weight of thousands of transformations — executives before boardrooms, grooms before weddings, fathers bringing sons for their first proper cut. We don't just trim hair; we mark occasions.

Our master barbers have trained for years to refine a skillset that blends traditional barbering techniques with modern precision. The result? A grooming experience that has earned us a 4.8-star rating from over 325 Google reviewers — including clients who've compared us to barbers across Europe.
